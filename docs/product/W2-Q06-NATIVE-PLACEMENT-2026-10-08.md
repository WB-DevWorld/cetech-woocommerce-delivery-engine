## Recovery successor — immutable original rejection and physical absence

Explicit Refresh and Confirm of a different quote can retire an original retry pointer after a known no-effect bind rejection without a binding, or a known no-effect verification rejection at prepared revision1. The read-only disposition requires the exact same owner, deterministic original placement ID, terminal original linked receipts without publication/audit effects, a physically accepted unbound replacement, current database time and current source guards. Namespace locks are globally sorted. Original physical native facts, quote rows and binding state remain fixed through the acknowledged read, rollback and connection retirement. Missing, pending, malformed, contradictory or unknown outcomes refuse; no binding revision is synthesized and no order, quote or receipt history is rewritten.

The native bridge captures the original order before owned SQL and repeats its raw and physical fences. An unbound order requires actual absence of protected quote ownership in native root and line metadata, including unknown quote-prefixed fields and malformed snapshot envelopes; stale loaded metadata cannot authorize release. A dedicated inert line key alone remains compatible with the unbound case. A prepared-one order uses its authentic saved protected packet and item mapping, without claiming verification or sealing. Owned SQL invokes no native getter or Woo callback.

The operation coordinator replays the first rejected receipt at reservation, effect-lock and rejection-recording boundaries, so an ordinary original retry or a concurrent refusal cannot overwrite the rejection after a pointer is released.
