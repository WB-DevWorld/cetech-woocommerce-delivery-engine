## Intended shopper and operator behavior

A merchant configures a service's preparation, dispatch, transport, buffer and final-mile rules with their actual calendars, closures and cutoffs. The customer sees an explainable delivery range to the selected endpoint. “Same Day” must actually fit the selected local day; a service name or a raw hour range cannot establish that fact. Air/Sea arrival at a port is not doorstep delivery. Missing data produces a truthful unavailable estimate or disables the affected service according to its required policy.

The original accepted promise remains part of order history. Later shipment estimates may change with an authorized reason and audit record, while preserving that original commitment. Calculation must not change Woo product prices, quote money, inventory, shipment state or capacity reservations.

All contracts, thresholds and semantics below are **proposed decisions for acceptance**, not claims about current runtime. Accepting the proposal can authorize the first bounded implementation checkpoint; later checkpoints retain their own reviewed handoffs.

| `PromiseInput` | One captured server UTC evaluation instant; site/owner/material group identity; actual origin/destination endpoint and effective service-policy receipt; explicit service-day constraint anchor (captured local date or known payment event); bounded deduplicated calendars; any required capacity observation; timezone-data/runtime identity. Browser dates, labels or correlation IDs provide no authority. |
| `PromiseResult` | Closed result format and state; governing policy/calendar/source identities; exact anchor type/known instant; component min/max facts and graph digest; either absolute UTC bounds with explicit display zone, or relative bounds awaiting a named event; acceptance deadline if applicable; safe reason codes; captured provenance. |

GLOBAL → PRODUCT → VARIATION resolution reuses the effective resolver and explicit source receipts. Labels never determine delivery truth. ## Time, anchors and duration units

The server captures `evaluated_at` once before calculation. Policy effectiveness and calendar versions are selected as of that instant. Stored absolute bounds are UTC; local rules and rendering use the captured IANA timezone and timezone-data identity. No PHP default timezone, browser clock or WordPress display locale becomes the calendar authority.

| Proposed anchor | Calculation and disclosure | Later event behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `order_accepted` | Capture a possible native acceptance interval `[evaluated_at, accept_until)`, ending no later than the original 300-second quote expiry, cutoff, policy/source validity and feasible service deadline. Compute a conservative completion envelope over that interval; do not pretend preparation began when a shopper merely viewed a quote. | Final trusted acceptance must lie within the captured interval and the frozen envelope remain feasible. Retry cannot slide either interval or completion window. A missed boundary requires explicit new review. |
| `checkout_capture` | Only a service contract explicitly starting its clock at captured quote issue may use that instant as its absolute anchor. It is not the default for order/payment-started preparation. | Retain the recorded window; missed feasibility cannot silently shift it forward. |
| `payment_confirmed` | Before the actual Woo payment event, retain a relative window and show “after payment confirmation”. Do not invent absolute dates from checkout time. A pure calendar transfer rule and all governing versions are captured in the accepted receipt. | The pre-gateway guard first verifies required policy/capacity conditions; the actual payment event cannot retroactively authorize a refused gateway. Once the authorized event is known, derive a separate current prediction from the frozen rule and actual event. An unresolved post-payment prediction is explicitly unavailable and does not erase a legitimate Woo payment record. Original relative commitment remains unchanged; no live-policy substitution or second payment effect. |

`order_accepted` means the successful authoritative Q06 final placement/seal event, recorded from its acknowledged receipt. Q05 quote Confirm and `QuoteStoredRow.accepted_at` occur earlier and are not preparation-start authority. The actual final event/source is recorded independently in the accepted order receipt. The three proposed anchor kinds are explicit. Other anchors require a separately versioned contract. For `order_accepted`, prove calendar-transfer monotonicity and conservatively bound all possible accepted instants; if that proof or a feasible interval is unavailable, refuse the absolute result. Equality at `accept_until` is too late. The promised range is frozen before acceptance, while the actual native acceptance event is recorded independently. Saved-order payment for promise-enabled absolute-window orders must obey a specifically captured late-payment rule; expired feasibility must refuse or require an explicit new reviewed path, without rewriting the original order or quote. Legacy v1 order-pay retains its qualified behavior. Selecting a promise policy must declare whether payment delay affects eligibility; it cannot imply that an unpaid order has already entered preparation.

Proposed units are `elapsed_minutes`, `calendar_days`, `business_minutes` and `business_days`. They are distinct typed values, not guessed from “days”. Elapsed minutes advance absolute time. Calendar days advance local dates preserving wall time, with explicit timezone conversion. Business minutes consume only declared open intervals. Business days first resolve the eligible start date/opening, then count strictly later eligible local dates (exclude-start convention), preserving the selected local time then moving to the next permitted opening if needed; zero days consumes zero eligible dates. A Friday request after cutoff that first resolves to Monday, plus one business day, completes on Tuesday under a Monday–Friday calendar. An include-start interpretation would require a separately named unit/version, not a hidden off-by-one. A service requiring a complete working-day quantum must express business minutes rather than assuming an eight-hour day.

Weekly intervals are half-open `[open, close)` and normalized, nonoverlapping and within one local date; overnight work is split into two explicit intervals. Completion may equal a recorded close; a new start or cutoff admission at close is not allowed. A nonexistent IANA wall time in a DST gap is unavailable; an arbitrary offset cannot make it real. A merchant may instead publish a distinct dated UTC interval whose semantics are explicit, rather than normalize that nonexistent local occurrence. A repeated local time requires an explicit occurrence/fold or offset that round-trips exactly to the declared IANA wall time. Calculation never silently chooses the PHP normalization. Two bounds use the same frozen timezone rules; lower must not exceed upper. A known zero-length promise uses equal bounds explicitly, unlike C05's older optional interval representation.

## Calendar, cutoff and graph algorithm

1. Validate the complete closed input and all referenced immutable records; reject a missing required component/calendar/authority, invalid timezone, cycle, missing edge, conflicting service or exceeded budget before emitting a winning result.
2. Apply ordering/admission cutoffs in their declared calendar timezone. Proposed equality rule: `local_start >= cutoff` misses that service window. A policy may explicitly select the next eligible window for ordinary service; a same-day constraint instead returns ineligible if no same-day window remains. Preserve reason and the chosen next-start facts.
3. In topological order, each node starts at the maximum completion of its predecessors and the next opening permitted for that node. Compute lower and upper separately using the chosen unit and frozen calendar. Explicit dispatch/handover waits and configured customs/handling buffers remain their own components.
4. Sequential paths carry the preceding completion forward, rather than adding labels. Parallel required branches start from their dependencies and join with `max(lower completions)` and `max(upper completions)`; they are not summed. A graph completing several endpoints reports them independently unless policy explicitly defines a joint completion. Missing final mile cannot be displayed as customer doorstep delivery.
5. Check the full conservative upper completion against operating and endpoint constraints. Express's configured closing-minus-two-hour cutoff and the actual upper-window feasibility are two checks. Do not shorten an uncertain upper bound to fit closing time. Same Day/Next Day constrain completion to local day 0/1 relative to the selected anchor in the policy's explicit promise timezone; a 6–24/24–48-hour marketing range does not override this constraint. For `order_accepted`, cap `accept_until` at the next local midnight so the captured interval cannot cross its service-date anchor. A 23:58 issue with a would-be 00:03 expiry must refuse acceptance at 00:00 and require a new quote; Next Day then refers to the next day after the actual seal date. Explicit capture-start services instead retain their declared capture-date semantics.
6. Apply any required capacity observation and produce a complete result or a typed refusal. Derive a conservative acceptance deadline from cutoff, policy effectiveness, capacity validity and promise feasibility. A future date clock must not extend price TTL, issue replay expiry or capacity validity.

Proposed initial limits: 16 graph nodes/32 edges, 16 referenced calendars per policy, eight opening intervals per date, 366 explicit closure/exception dates per calendar version, 730 local-date lookahead per calculation and 100,000 total calendar steps per complete cart. Individual policy/calendar records are at most 32 KiB. Existing quote 200-line/200-group, 64 KiB/depth-16/4,096-node and C04 16 KiB/depth-5/128-node limits are unchanged; richer records are referenced, not injected into a rule body. The complete new quote/snapshot packet must fit its reviewed limits or the entire result refuses. No partial group winner or hidden truncation. P01 must freeze measured, justified limits before code adoption; a larger commercial horizon requires an explicit revision rather than an unbounded loop.

Policy/calendar records are bulk loaded once per unique reference outside owned quote SQL. Calculation is pure and performs no network/WordPress callbacks while quote/control-plane locks are held. Required native facts are verified at their proper admitted boundaries. Actual step/query/lock observations, not an assumed complexity claim, qualify P06.

## Capacity, failures and authority

`capacity_mode=none` is explicit nonreservation operation. If capacity is configured as required, a registered adapter must return a site/service/endpoint/window-bound, revisioned observation with source identity and validity: available, unavailable or unknown. Unavailable/unknown/stale/wrong-source data makes that service ineligible; a missing adapter is not unlimited capacity. For a payment-relative offer, required window-bound capacity cannot be authorized until that future window is bounded and actually observed; generic current availability is insufficient. Unknown future capacity therefore refuses such an offer in the first release. Nonbinding availability is revalidated on Confirm and final admission. It must never be advertised as a reserved slot.

A real capacity hold/reservation/confirmation protocol is a separate design boundary, including ownership, concurrency, expiry and release. This proposal does not manufacture such authority from quote budget slots. The first proposed release supports payment-relative Standard/Relaxed/custom service clocks, while literal Same Day/Next Day require an already bounded absolute anchor. A payment-relative same/next-day service remains unsupported until a separately accepted event-admission/capacity contract establishes its day feasibility. Quote price TTL, promise acceptance deadline, capacity observation validity and retention grace remain distinct. A service requiring a binding hold cannot be enabled until its actual adopter and physical proofs exist.

States are `absolute_window`, `relative_window`, `unavailable` and `ineligible`. Optional unavailable estimates show an honest “delivery estimate unavailable” without a fake date. A required unavailable/ineligible promise suppresses that service and refuses stale submitted selections. Turning a setting off cannot allow an already managed promise order into legacy payment fallback. Unauthorized callers get safe responses without promise, quote or native order disclosure. Operational exceptions preserve the original accepted receipt and use C07 pause/recovery controls.

## Reader-first quote and order integration

The initial pure contracts are isolated from the retained `legacy_fixed_base_v1` provider. A later promise-enabled provider/profile and explicitly distinct closed terms/context/snapshot formats carry structured promise receipts. Existing v1 bytes, digests and format semantics are never amended in place; old rows/orders remain readable as captured relative text or missing estimate. Required promise support also needs an explicit new C05 component/readiness contract; optional v1 reference/from/until fields are insufficient and currently refuse `required=true`.

First mount strict forward readers for both legacy and proposed new formats with new writes OFF. Prove unsupported future versions preserve original bytes and refuse authority; do not fall back to weaker format readers. Only afterward enable new-format writes behind an explicit revisioned protected adoption decision. Forward additive persistence/version targets must be frozen in P02/P04 after inspecting actual schema; this design does not declare or apply schema 10.

On new issue, bind the full promise result/policy/calendar/capacity receipt to exact quote material. Confirm and final placement revalidate current required facts and feasibility. Policy/calendar/capacity changes or missed deadlines require new explicit review; original replay cannot recalculate a new window, retime expiry or swap source versions. Price and tax authority remain the existing native captured receipt.

The order stager must copy accepted per-group promise facts into its physically verified mandatory packet, derive public line estimates from that packet and seal it before payment/free completion. Required C05 authority, accepted body digest, saved physical bytes and final payment continuation all agree. Client/session summary text and current offers are never substituted for those saved facts. HPOS/CPT item mapping remains exact. Historical reads after policy/calendar deletion, current timezone changes or different locale use recorded original facts; known relative histories stay relative and are not retroactively converted to dates.

Shipment creation receives a structured original promise from the acknowledged snapshot. `ShipmentEtaService` continues to own a separately audited current estimate. Refunds, manual operational edits, later payment events and shipment exceptions cannot change original promise or monetary history. Native retention must include any new policy/promise references before authorizing their deletion; the present unknown-reference default remains conservative.

## Customer and operator surfaces

PDP incomplete-destination output is explicitly a preliminary estimate. Classic, Blocks and Store API consume the same authorized view and show service, explainable ETA/relative anchor and fee consistently. No PDP preview may mint an accepted promise or claim a precise endpoint without required destination facts. Server time is authoritative; client countdowns provide display only. Localized text derives from structured facts and translatable reason codes, with captured original text retained for history.

Native admin editing separates service identity, policy version, calendars and assignment. Preview exposes effective source and component explanation to authorized staff without publishing private origin/economics. Published versions are immutable; edits create a new draft/version with author/reason. Optimistic concurrency, scoped C04/C03 command receipts, effective-boundary races and permission failures must be proved. No global service/calendar demo defaults, country assumptions or auto-enabled services are introduced on activation.
