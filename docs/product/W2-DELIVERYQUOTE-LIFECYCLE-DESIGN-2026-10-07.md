## What this adds

Today the engine calculates a delivery price, but the result has no retained quote ID, expiry or acceptance receipt. This design gives a shopper a delivery price that can be identified and reviewed for five minutes. A repeated request returns the same quote. A material change or expiry asks the shopper to refresh and review delivery again. A saved order keeps the actual accepted facts even after prices and rules change.

Wave 1 C01–C07 supplies identity, safe explanations, transactional completion, versioned rules, historical readers, data lifecycle and emergency controls. This package uses those foundations and the existing RateQuoteEngine. It does not complete the rest of Wave 2: promise calendars, delivery promotions, private cost estimation and currency conversion retain their own work packages. All twelve DE-QUOTE requirements remain historically PARTIAL_IMPLEMENTATION in the frozen registry until actual implementation and evidence justify a separately reviewed update.

## Aggregate and private facts

The first adopter issues one quote for the complete current owned cart attempt, with a bounded managed-group manifest and per-group money/provenance terms. Distinct supported groups can have different origins/services; each group must satisfy its homogeneous provider rule. Unmanaged residual charges stay native and participate in final native context verification. Admission counts one cart issuance, not every package. One quote binds one placement/order; protected line/package facts refer to its UUID plus their opaque component key.

An issued quote comprises an immutable header/body plus guarded lifecycle state. The header has format/profile version, UUID, site, principal/session digest, purpose, material-context digest, body digest, issue/accept/invalidate operation namespace hashes, created/expiry UTC instants and revision. The private body contains a bounded sorted member set, exact product/variation-parent relationship, stable native cart-line binding, quantity, source-route/configuration evidence, availability/choice/offer/service, resolved origin/supplier/profile tuple, destination-zone and full versioned delivery-location digest, current inventory/eligibility evidence and provider provenance.

Precise addresses stay with Woo; quote storage records necessary scoped IDs and purpose-separated keyed destination/session digests, not an extra raw address copy. Hashes remain private. Key/salt rotation invalidates outstanding applicability; it does not rewrite historical snapshots. Locale labels are separately snapshotted for display unless the provider declares them material to price. A quoted label is not a promise/ETA.

Money contains distinct list charge, delivery promotion/subsidy receipt, final ex-tax charge, native per-rate taxes and rounded total/display facts, currency/precision and the Woo tax context. Cost is `known(amount,currency,provider/version)` or `unavailable(reason)`; route measurements are either explicit typed values with units/provider/version or `not_recorded`. Unknown is never coerced to zero. The quote codec rejects exponent/junk/nonfinite/negative/overflow amounts and undeclared precision/currency. It validates the existing engine's output; it does not claim the engine's float operations were replaced.

Pricing policy provenance is a content-addressed immutable receipt: adapter/profile version plus complete bounded relevant legacy candidate content and chosen row/zone. Mutable `updated_at` is insufficient. A different candidate gaining precedence, or an edit within one second, changes the digest. Legacy rate inclusive `effective_to` is preserved; quote validity uses its own half-open interval.

### Material context

The server reconstructs context; it never trusts a submitted fingerprint, quote amount or group ID. Canonical group members are sorted by full stable line identity; reversing members preserves equality, adding/removing/changing quantity does not. It includes exact variation parent, full destination digest, service/choice/endpoint, routed legacy/ECR source and relevant current revisions, resolved dimensions, inventory eligibility, complete pricing candidate/policy receipt, native currency/taxability/rates/exemption/rounding context and actual selected native delivery money. Same SKU to different destinations remains distinct.

`CartDeliverySelectionFingerprint` and V1/V2 group IDs are components, not substitutes: they omit quantity and several material facts. No new raw segment is appended to existing group IDs. A quote ID uses a separately declared rate/session binding and protected metadata. Unknown source dimensions differ from authored absent dimensions. A positive card scope with absent/unproved request scope refuses retention, rather than silently changing the matcher or picking a new tariff.

## State and replay

| Stored state | Meaning / transition |
|---|---|
| issued | Immutable verified price; may accept once before expiry with equal current context. |
| accepted | Immutable acceptance receipt with accepted_at and accepted revision; still needs current applicability for new placement/payment. |
| invalidated | Material context no longer matches; original body/amount retained. Replacement has a new UUID and new shopper review. |
| expired | Derived immediately from clock even if maintenance has not written state; terminal acceptance refusal. Acceptance history remains readable. |
| stripped | Terminal never-accepted payload removed under D13; minimal header explains expired/unavailable replay, no calculation or renewed TTL. |

Placement is a separate binding state `prepared -> sealed`, with failed/unconfirmed reconciliation. It does not overload quote status. An accepted quote expires for new use while its accepted historical receipt remains accepted evidence. A valid unchanged quote stays byte-identical. A failed current read is unavailable, not proof of a material change; do not overwrite accepted facts on uncertainty.

Each quote has one server-minted accept envelope: UUID/body digest/opened revision/original absolute expiry and token namespace. Client retries send only the same opaque quote reference and a separately declared opaque acceptance handle. The authorized server resolves the immutable body digest/opened revision/absolute expiry and operation namespace from its private header; those internal hashes are never sent as shopper command fields. The handle is purpose-bound retry input, not authorization. Expired create replay returns its original expired identity, never a refreshed calculation; same issue token with changed canonical intent conflicts. Accepted replay returns the recorded receipt, then reports whether it is currently applicable. It does not admit another order. A different accept token is not a way to renew or reaccept the quote. New review produces a new quote/envelope.

C01 authority/identity and C03 authorization run before stored lookup, effects, replay and disclosure. A guest requires the verified current Woo session; a logged-in customer requires both current user and cart-session binding. Logout/login, cart/session rotation and cross-site access require reissue. A WordPress guest nonce is CSRF protection, not customer authority. Ordinary reads/PDP estimates create no C03 reservation. Creation and acceptance completions/events contain only allowlisted UUID/digests/revision/state, never money/address/private cost bodies. Lost acknowledgement stays unconfirmed until the original namespace reconciles; unsent commit rolls back. No second provider effect, audit or acceptance follows a successful replay.

Quote profile registration is a new finite customer-purpose C03 profile, not reuse of admin capabilities. Profile intent validates the sealed server facts before the C03 unit; final in-unit authorization and source fences verify them again. Production registry stays unused by checkout until its adopter checkpoint. C03's existing record/event codec limits remain unchanged. A schema9 claim is made only when Q02 creates and physically verifies the new tables; this design leaves schema8 unchanged.

Total owned lock order: coordinator-owned operation namespace first (as existing C03 actually locks it), then any exact referenced C03 namespaces sorted, C07 current control row when required, declared source-table fences in sorted table/key order, exact quote budget slot when required, quote header, exact placement-reference slot. Cleanup locks the stored issue/accept/invalidate namespaces including missing-key gaps before proving absence. It never scans all completion JSON. Retention never asks generic C03 to sort its own namespace together with referenced producers. Producer profiles never acquire a retention namespace or reciprocal producer-reference chain; retention is the only operation locking those referenced namespaces together. Producers otherwise follow the same target order. Distinct issue/accept/invalidate purpose hashes prevent aliasing. The one accept namespace per quote makes reference lookup finite. Readiness verifies indexes, types, InnoDB and current prefix; missing/corrupt/multiple identity refuses.

Expensive resolver/Woo/filter/provider work is outside short owned locks. The final unit compares fresh immutable captured receipts and declared current SQL source fences; it does not call C07 `confirm_enabled()` inside C03, because that helper opens/retires another session. Acceptance checks `EmergencyControlStore::current` on the same OperationSession. Final C07 admission remains separate. No quote read grants a capacity/stock reservation or a serializable lock on all Woo inventory.

The first retained legacy provider obtains a bounded complete rate-candidate view on its owned session. The proposed schema9 migration includes a named additive rate-card range index `(delivery_offer_id,destination_zone_id,currency_code,id)`; all statuses in relevant ranges are captured, with next-key current locks preventing edits/insertion during the final policy comparison. Existing rate calculations consume the captured complete active rows through a bounded repository adapter. No legacy formula/matcher/writer is changed. Configuration source fences and final native context checks remain explicit. If a source cannot supply a verifiable fence, it is unsupported. Before/after hashes alone are not advertised as atomic prevention of a concurrent write. Q04 includes actual two-connection preopened-snapshot, edit and insertion proofs plus query-plan/lock-budget evidence; no stronger guarantee is claimed until they pass.

Budget admission is a dedicated native **pre-C03-reservation gate**, not a profile budget check after generic C03 has already inserted pending. After current session/shape authorization it current-locks exact site/session minute slots in fixed site-then-session order, increments both counters once, and atomically records a private single-use issue lease keyed by the original issuance namespace/intent. Over-budget fresh requests create no C03 row, lease or quote. Only a positive gate receipt may enter the issue profile; the profile validates it again and atomically consumes that lease alongside quote/event/completion. These operational counters/lease are not a shopper acceptance or quote-created completion. This gate uses its own declared native owned session and honest unsent-versus-lost-ack reconciliation; a failed acknowledgement never runs capture until the original exact slot is confirmed. It makes no generic C03 behavior change.

Only the current server attempt that owns the lease may capture. Exact replays return completed quote facts or pending status, never concurrent capture. Lease expires after60 seconds, no renewal/takeover. A dead/uncertain capture reconciles its original envelope; conclusively no quote committed terminates unavailable, and a fresh explicit request obtains a new slot. Minimal admission intent/namespace tombstone survives to prevent stale-token resurrection. No automatic background provider retry. The first provider makes zero external calls. Q03 must prove gate-before-reservation, duplicate/expired-lease/counter/lost-ack behavior. The20/200 limits bound positive admissions/capture and new issuance reservations in this adopter; they do not establish a storage-size bound, nor protect unrelated existing C03 callers.

D13 requires a new explicit C06 quote retention profile. Scan at most100 candidates per invocation, finite high water, maximum2-second soft work budget, strict100-mutation ceiling; a database lock wait is2 seconds per acquisition, not a proven2-second total deadline. A stalled query stops work on timeout/error and records unavailable. Strip only `now >= expires_at +1800s`, never accepted, conclusively no pending/unconfirmed completion or prepared/sealed/history reference, exact original header/body/revision and current retention authority. Recheck under owned locks, write a typed strip event/completion, retain minimal header. Failure or unknown reference retains payload. Budget windows may delete after24 hours under their own exact profile; operation/admission/tombstone history is not age-deleted. Only expired minute counter rows are removed after24 hours, not admission namespace receipts. This preserves replay at the cost of growing minimal records; no total-size bound or generic C03 request-rate protection is claimed.

## Provider and native Woo adoption

The first real provider is `legacy_fixed_base_v1`. It supports every group member with the same complete resolved offer/service/choice/origin/supplier/profile/destination tuple, fixed_item or fixed_shipment, finite complete candidate set and verified exact no-conversion currency. It explicitly attests delivery-promotion `none`, list=final ex-tax, cost unavailable, route not recorded. It validates native Woo shipping money and tax receipt, including method tax_status rather than assuming unused offer tax_class controls taxes. Third-party shipping filters/coupons/currency changes that cannot be attributed and sealed refuse this subset. Explicit configured zero can be retained; missing money never becomes zero. Unmanaged residual shipping and native merchandise/taxes/payments remain Woo-owned.

No grouping/matcher/rate-card writer is rewritten to make an unsupported quote succeed. Historical COR020 split-versus-consolidation and tax/promotion allocation remain a separate owner tariff decision. A future provider can support additional contexts after its own contract and native evidence. The bounded provider can be useful without pretending to complete unknown private economics, multicurrency or pickup pricing.

Cart rates attach an opaque cart-quote/component binding while preserving existing parsable group identity. Server resolves it under current session; rate cache hits still require quote expiry/context/authority checks. Shipping recalculation never silently accepts a changed price: show a new reviewed quote. Classic and actual Blocks/Store API must use the same aggregate/service, finite errors and selection envelope. PDP estimates remain clearly separate. Guest endpoints use current server session/CSRF and atomic admission budgets; no anonymous quote-by-ID endpoint.

### Proposed COR029 placement boundary / C07 ordering

1. Resolve current cart/order candidate and quote ownership, money/tax and eligibility outside owned control locks. Store API GET/PATCH or provisional Classic line hooks may refresh draft facts; they do not accept or seal a quote.
2. On a final authorized Classic submit or StoreAPI POST, accept the exact cart quote and stage a placement binding with the actual order/complete managed-group logical membership. Neither a prepared binding nor snapshot presence admits payment.
3. Persist the accepted quote facts through HPOS-compatible Woo CRUD, reload and verify exact immutable snapshot/native digest, then record its verified context digest while the placement binding remains prepared. Classic staging runs at `woocommerce_checkout_order_created/PHP_INT_MAX-1`, before C07 freezes saved raw state at PHP_INT_MAX. StoreAPI final POST staging follows request synchronization and precedes its processed final guard; recognized quote context makes the old processed writer an idempotent verifier. GET/PATCH never seals. Interrupted or lost acknowledgement remains prepared/unconfirmed and blocks payment; original-envelope reconciliation verifies existing physical bytes without recomputing current prices.
4. Build the C07 local native binding from the now-sealed facts, then perform its final current-control confirmation. The trusted final coordinator verifies that exact admission, current-locks the same control revision and exact quote state/body/material/owner on the placement C03 session, verifies authoritative DB `now < expires_at`, and records and acknowledges the immutable sealed placement receipt, and returns to the paid/free branch. The receipt stays in the private binding table and is excluded from the already-frozen monetary inputs; it cannot authorize payment by itself. For quote-owned placement the earlier C07 stamp is tentative until this final acknowledged receipt; its commit is the final admission point. Expiry or a pause winning the final lock prevents placement, while a later pause/expiry does not undo that acknowledged continuation. A prior stamp cannot be silently refreshed after mutation. This trusted staging-to-binding phase must be explicit in the final orchestrator; any mutation after binding refuses before gateway/free-order processing.
5. Return the sealed facts for history. Later hooks cannot replace them from current config/cart. Re-entry with equal prepared facts is idempotent; changed drafts before seal may progress, changed sealed orders require a replacement checkout rather than overwriting history.

Classic `woocommerce_resume_order` runs before native pending-order item deletion: intercept an already-sealed quote-owned retry and route the authorized customer to native order-pay. Owned provisional drafts reconcile separately; no private order loader runs before Woo authorization. Classic Woo can delete/recreate item rows on a reused pending order; bindings therefore use logical line/group digest plus verified actual native item mapping, not item ID alone. If a quote-enabled order is already sealed, changed retry creates a new checkout order and preserves old facts. StoreAPI can update an existing pending order even on GET/PATCH: before publishing the final placement receipt, detach its mutable own-session draft pointer with an acknowledged/recoverable session disposition and retain a private payment-continuation identity. Later GET/PATCH cannot mutate the sealed order; same-envelope final retry resolves its original binding rather than creating a duplicate. Failure to confirm pointer disposition blocks the continuation until original-envelope reconciliation. Native Woo session/nonce authorization precedes private order loading; generic early REST pre-dispatch hooks are insufficient. Q06 must cover these actual pinned hooks and order reuse, not merely mock `order_created`.

Reader-before-writer: introduce a dedicated mandatory-for-quote-enabled-placement versioned `delivery_quote` snapshot contract, not C05's unrelated `quote_policy.reference`. Its exact allowed facts include quote UUID/profile/body/material digests, issue/expiry/acceptance instants, sealed placement UUID and declared money/provenance receipt. Protected private facts and public display projections are distinct. New readers recognize V1/V2 legacy, known quote format, unknown/corrupt format as unsupported; missing new fields cannot silently downgrade a quote-owned order to legacy. Protected reader keys are `format`, `quote_id`, `profile`, `issued_at`, `expires_at`, `accepted_at`, `body_digest`, `material_digest`, `placement_id`, `context_digest`, `money_receipt` and `provenance_receipt`, with strict nested codecs rather than arbitrary provider JSON. `context_digest` hashes a versioned canonical tuple of actual order/logical-item coordinates, quote/body/material references, accepted terms and protected core snapshot/native money facts, explicitly excluding its own field and private final placement/outcome data. It is installed before the saved freeze; verification never writes a self-hash or adds it after stamping. Admission also resolves the private exact binding; snapshot presence is not sealed-placement proof. Register all HPOS/item references and protect history. Write readiness precedes activation. Reader format/version and new metadata are Q05's bounded contract, implemented before Q06 writes.

Rollback disables new issuance/adoption and retains forward schema/readers/sealed-order validation. Rolling binaries behind the quote reader is refused for quote-owned unpaid orders; paid history remains readable with the supported reader. No destructive down migration or history rewrite is a rollback mechanism. This is a proposed placement contract, not a claim that old COR029 is already closed.

## Shopper and administrator outcomes

Finite shopper DTO: opaque quote ID, status/current applicability, expiry, customer label, safe list/promotion/final/display money and currency, safe recovery code/action. No RateQuoteResult::toArray, raw context, address/destination hash, origin/supplier IDs, rate-card code, internal policy JSON, estimated cost/margin or provider diagnostics. Guest/public shared caches and URLs never carry private quote bodies. Admin diagnostics require current capability, exact quote scope and per-section private authority before load and again before disclosure.

Proposed copy: “Delivery price is held until {time} while your delivery details and price rules stay unchanged.”; “This delivery quote has expired. Refresh delivery and review the new price.”; “Your delivery details changed. Review delivery again.”; “We could not confirm this quote. Retry the same request.”; “Delivery quoting is temporarily unavailable. Try again.” C07 keeps its existing paused outcome. Preserve cart choices and destination on failure; show the refreshed amount before another acceptance. A timer assists the shopper; the server clock decides validity. The technical review is owner+agent review, with no external person's availability gate.
