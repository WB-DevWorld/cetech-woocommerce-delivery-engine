It does not name a seventh quote checkpoint. Prepare that next bounded design proposal after the current conformance review; do not invent Q07, implement its runtime or silently enable a site.

## Concrete adoption sequence

1. Complete/review the present 48-obligation and 12-row conformance closeout and record remaining gaps without changing frozen classifications. Choose a narrow supported fixed-base pilot catalog and exclude unproved FX/promo/source/tariff contexts and external object-cache configurations; all missing ultimate/Stable features need not block a deliberately bounded pilot. Record production retention/reference adoption as a planned product follow-up with exact native HPOS/item absence authority and preservation/race proofs. Current generic retention fixtures must not be called production cleanup.
2. Declare target staging/pilot site, database, versions, permitted writes, fixture/data ownership, prohibited effects and cleanup. Record real current flags/options/cache implementation. Before any pilot adoption, accept the explicit absence of automatic retention and set a bounded duration/traffic budget, row/payload-growth and privacy monitoring, pause/stop thresholds and responsible operator. If that interim boundary is unacceptable for the target, deliver qualified native retention first. Back up database/files, record source/package identity and affected products, prepare a known forward-reader rollback artifact and cache/session recovery plan. Do not use destructive SQL cleanup as the interim plan.
3. Build the exact accepted immutable package and verify extracted contents/checksum. On representative staging, qualify real target theme, HPOS/checkout modes, customer/variation/multi-package paths and whichever B2B/FX/cache/payment integrations are actually included. Rehearse retained-data forward schema upgrade and rollback/disable with quote history/readers retained. Do not roll behind Q05 readers or destructively down-migrate quote history.
4. Obtain owner/staff physical UX and operational acceptance and explicit site/pilot authorization. Verify staff can explain Refresh/Confirm, safe refusals/retry/order-pay, acknowledged versus uncertain states and history preservation; live payment effects need their own authorized test plan.
5. With native Delivery shipping method and complete Classic/Blocks upstream setup ready, explicitly enable the revisioned delivery-price-review adoption setting through its protected native admin action. Turning it off is the preferred new-adoption rollback; protected orders/readers remain.
6. Run the monitored 10–25-product pilot before broad catalog rollout. Monitor add-to-cart/checkout failures, shipping mismatch, duplication/missing shipments, privacy, theme/currency issues, recovery and quote/budget-row/private-payload growth. Stop at the agreed duration/traffic/growth limits; disabling new adoption must preserve existing order/quote history. Broader rollout/release is a separate decision after target results and an operational retention decision.

The distinction is practical: CI proves the retained implementation in its declared environment; staging proves the chosen target stack; owner physical acceptance proves the workflow; explicit authorization permits live adoption. No earlier green receipt supplies those later decisions.
