## Scene 1 — Normal workflow

**What is visible:** Delivery Settings.

**Narration:** Delivery Settings is the normal current workflow for RC.2. That is where everyday staff work.

**Action being performed:** Show Delivery Settings home.

**Key teaching point:** One everyday system.

## Scene 2 — Legacy area

**What is visible:** Legacy Delivery Rules screen and any warning/copy.

**Narration:** Legacy Delivery Rules is an older compatibility and migration area. It is not a second everyday delivery system. Normal staff should not edit it unless an administrator authorises a migration task.

**Action being performed:** Open Legacy Rules; highlight warning if present; do not edit.

**Key teaching point:** Legacy = leave alone unless authorised.

## Scene 3 — Boundary

**What is visible:** Return to Delivery Settings.

**Narration:** If you are unsure which screen to use, choose Delivery Settings and ask. Do not maintain the same rule in two places.

**Action being performed:** Navigate back to Delivery Settings.

**Key teaching point:** No dual systems for daily work.
