Help

Simple guidance for setting prices and checking checkout.

How delivery pricing works

Delivery pricing works in three steps:

  1. Create a Delivery Area for where you deliver.
  2. Create a Delivery Option for the service you provide.
  3. Create a Delivery Charge to set the price for that area and option.

Example City centre + Standard Delivery = GHS 25.00

How to check checkout

  1. Make sure you have at least one zone, offer, and active rate card.
  2. Open your store and add a product to the cart.
  3. Go to checkout and enter a delivery address inside one of your zones.
  4. Confirm a delivery fee appears before placing the order.
